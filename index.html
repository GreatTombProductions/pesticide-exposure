About this tool

California is the only U.S. state that requires reporting of every commercial agricultural pesticide application. The data goes back to 1990. This tool uses 2023 data covering 138 million lbs of pesticides across 22,900+ square-mile sections.

How it works: Your address is geocoded to coordinates, then we find all PLSS (Public Land Survey System) sections within your chosen radius. Each section is approximately 1 square mile. We show what pesticides were reported in those sections during 2023.

What the toxicity classes mean:

• POISON/DANGER — Signal word for the most acutely toxic products. Restricted-use pesticides often carry this label.
• DANGER — Causes irreversible eye damage or skin corrosion.
• WARNING — Moderately toxic through one or more exposure routes.
• CAUTION — Slightly toxic or relatively non-toxic.
• NONE — No signal word required (minimum risk).

Limitations: Geographic precision is ~1 square mile (PLSS section). Urban structural pest control applications often lack PLSS coordinates and are excluded. This tool shows reported applications, not measured environmental exposure.

Data source: California DPR Pesticide Use Reports (2023). PLSS geographic data from CalPIP.
